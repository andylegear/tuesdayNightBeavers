All meals are provided — dinner on Friday evening, s'mores and toasted marshmallows at the campfire, and breakfast on Saturday morning.

If your child has any allergies, dietary requirements, or food intolerances, please let us know in advance so we can make sure they're well looked after.

🏷️ Label Everything!

Please write your child's name on all their belongings — sleeping bag, pillow, hoodie, torch, bag, the lot! Things get mixed up very easily when 20+ excited Beavers are camping together. A permanent marker on a luggage tag works great.

❓ Questions from Parents

Here are the answers to the questions we get asked most often. If you have any other questions, don't hesitate to get in touch!

What if my child gets scared or homesick?

No problem at all — and it's nothing to worry about. We'll phone you straight away and you can come and collect them. They'll still have had a great time up to that point! Most of them are so excited that they don't want to leave.

What if the weather is bad?

The camp goes ahead regardless! If the weather turns really bad, we move everything inside the Scout Hall. We've done this before and the Beavers love it either way — sometimes indoor camping is even more of an adventure.

Will my child be supervised all night?

Yes. All four leaders — Joe, Andy, Alina, and Amy — will be present for the entire overnight event. There is always a leader awake and on duty.

What time is drop-off and collection?

Drop-off: 7:00 PM on Friday evening at the Scout Hall.
Collection: 10:00 AM on Saturday morning at the Scout Hall.

Does my child need camping experience?

Not at all! We practise tent pitching at our regular Tuesday night meetings in the weeks beforehand, so they'll already know what they're doing. The leaders help with everything.

What about medication?

If your child takes any medication, please label it clearly with their name and dosage, and hand it directly to a leader at drop-off. Let us know in advance if possible.

Is there an extra cost?

No. The overnight camp is covered under your child's existing membership fees. There's nothing extra to pay.

What if I have more questions?

Just ask! Contact Andy at andylegear@gmail.com or call/text 086 450 0112. We're always happy to chat.
